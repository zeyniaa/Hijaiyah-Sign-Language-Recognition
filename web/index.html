RECOGNITION GUIDE

One clear gesture at a time.

Face the camera in even light. Keep your right hand, shoulder and elbow in view. Hold the gesture steadily while the app collects 20 frames.

  1. Capture. Enable the camera or upload a short video.
  2. Inspect. MediaPipe draws the detected hand landmarks.
  3. Recognize. A trained Transformer maps the sequence to a letter and harakat.

Install the project’s vision requirements. Add checkpoints/transformer_70_30_best.h5 and, when training used a custom class order, checkpoints/class_names.json. Restart after changing the checkpoint. No prediction is generated without trained weights.
